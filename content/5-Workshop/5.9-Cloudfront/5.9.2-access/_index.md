#### 5.9.2. Access Your Live Application

After clicking create, you will be redirected to the distribution's detail page. 

1. Look for the **Distribution domain name**. It will look something like `https://d123456789.cloudfront.net`.
2. Check the **Last modified** status. It will show as `Deploying`. 
3. Wait approximately 3 to 5 minutes until the deployment is complete (a date and time will appear).
![Cloudfront](images/Screenshot4.png)
Once deployed, copy the CloudFront Domain Name and open it in a new browser tab. 

**Congratulations!** 🎉 Your Serverless Real-time Chat App is now live on the Internet, fully secure, and ready to scale to millions of users!
